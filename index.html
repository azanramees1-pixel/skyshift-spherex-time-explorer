Here’s the exciting part

The comet brightened

NASA reports that 3I/ATLAS brightened significantly in SPHEREx’s December observations, around two months after it passed closest to the Sun.

A clue in the coma

The fuzzy cloud around a comet is called its coma. It contains gas and dust released as the comet warms.

How Skyshift works

01 / FIND

Choose a place or object

Search the SPHEREx archive for a sky position, object, or moving body. The archive lists images, observation dates, and wavelength coverage.

02 / COMPARE

Move through time

Put observations in date order. A simple slider helps people notice differences and decide which part of the sky to look at more closely.

03 / CHECK

Follow the evidence

Show the image source, date, wavelength, and data quality. A visual change is a clue for people to investigate, not proof of a discovery.
